Representative Tasks

Hidden Dependencies

Critical evidence may appear in comments, archived bundles, policy appendices, historical notes, spreadsheet formulas, or metadata files that are not called out in the task instruction.

Rubric Examples

Rubrics check whether an agent used all required evidence, respected policy constraints, reconciled conflicting values, documented exceptions, and delivered a final artifact that can be audited.
